Daniela Diaz Caro, MS, CGC

Genetic Counselor · Researcher · Educator

My work focuses on genetic counseling, genomics, health equity, and expanding access to genetic and genomic services. I am particularly interested in how research can improve communication, care, and outcomes across diverse communities.

Across my research, teaching, and collaborative work, I aim to develop approaches that make genetic counseling and genomic services more responsive to the communities they serve.
