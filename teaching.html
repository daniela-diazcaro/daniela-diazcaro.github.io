Teaching

Helping students become thoughtful researchers.

I teach and mentor genetic counseling students in research methods, research design, and critical thinking, with a focus on asking questions that matter.

Teaching & Mentoring

Research methods and design

Research faculty at the Mass General Brigham University of Health Professions, teaching genetic counseling students how to design studies, choose appropriate methods, and interpret evidence critically.

Research advising

Advising students through their research projects, from shaping a meaningful research question to analysis, writing, and dissemination.

Alumni and student writing retreat

A twice-monthly, two-hour virtual writing retreat for graduates and second-year students: protected, structured time to make steady progress on manuscripts, theses, and other writing.

International mentorship

Mentoring genetic counseling trainees outside the United States on research design and on building local research partnerships.
